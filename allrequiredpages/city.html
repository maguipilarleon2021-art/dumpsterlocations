Frequently Asked Questions

1. What happens if I overfill the container?

If materials exceed the fill line, our drivers may be unable to transport the load safely. You might be asked to remove the excess debris or pay an additional fee for a second haul.

2. How long can I keep the dumpster on my property?

Our standard rental period typically lasts seven to ten days, but we offer flexible extensions for long-term projects. Just contact us at least 24 hours before your scheduled pickup to adjust the date.

3. Do I need a permit for a dumpster?

If the bin stays on your private driveway, a permit is usually not required. However, if the container must sit on a public street, you may need to obtain a permit from the local municipality.

4. Are there weight limits for the bins?

Yes, each size has a specific weight capacity. Exceeding this limit often results in additional landfill charges, so we recommend discussing your material types with us during booking.

5. How soon can I expect delivery after I place my order?

We strive to offer next-day delivery for those who need to rent a dumpster in #State quickly. During peak seasons, we recommend booking at least two to three days in advance to ensure your preferred size is in stock. We always confirm a delivery window so you know exactly when to expect our team
